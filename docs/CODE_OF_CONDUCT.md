# Code of Conduct

*Adapted from the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1*

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and leaders, pledge to create a harassment-free experience for everyone in our community. This commitment extends to all individuals, regardless of factors such as age, body size, visible or invisible disabilities, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation or any attribute, characteristic or condition.

We are dedicated to fostering an open, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community by acting and interacting in ways that promote respect and kindness.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior that contributes to a welcoming environment includes:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Providing and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for our actions and learning from mistakes.
- Prioritizing the overall community's well-being, not just individual preferences.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Usage of sexualized language or imagery, or making sexual advances.
- Engaging in trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Harassing individuals publicly or privately.
- Disclosing others' private information without explicit consent.
- Exhibiting conduct that would be deemed inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Our community leaders are entrusted with enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior. They will address inappropriate, offensive, threatening, or harmful conduct by taking fair and appropriate corrective measures.

Community leaders hold the authority to edit, remove, or reject comments, code contributions, edits, issues, and other forms of contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Whenever necessary, they will communicate the reasons behind their moderation decisions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces. It also applies when individuals officially represent the community in public settings. Examples of representation include using official email addresses, posting from official social media accounts, or acting as representatives at online or offline events.

## Enforcement

Incidents of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior should be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at codeofconduct [@] morteo.mx. We will promptly and fairly review and investigate all complaints.

All community leaders are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of incident reporters.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will adhere to the following Community Impact Guidelines when determining consequences for violations of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction**
   - Community Impact: Use of inappropriate language or behavior.
   - Consequence: A private, written warning from community leaders. A public apology may be requested.

2. **Warning**
   - Community Impact: A violation through a single incident or actions.
   - Consequence: A warning with potential consequences for continued behavior.

3. **Temporary Ban**
   - Community Impact: A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior.
   - Consequence: A temporary ban from interactions or communication with the community.

4. **Permanent Ban**
   - Community Impact: Demonstrating a pattern of serious violations.
   - Consequence: A permanent ban from public interaction within the community.
